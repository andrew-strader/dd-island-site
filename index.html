About

Hello! We're DD Island. We make psych-infused, twangy folk rock in Brooklyn. We've spent the last few years playing shows with our friends in New York and along the east coast. During that time, we've also refined a collection of songs that'll be released as our first full-length record, Setting Sun, in spring 2026. A lot of love and hard work went into crafting this album, so we hope you'll take the time to check out our music, attend a show, or support us by purchasing some merch.

We are:

Brandon Rhodes — Vocals, Guitar
Andrew Strader — Bass
Zach Litchman — Drums
Jeremy Batt — Guitar, Steel
Spenser Naess — Guitar, Steel
